Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

General principles

Stagia takes protecting your privacy seriously. The GigFlow Live, InBeat, and GigFlow Editor apps run entirely offline — your songs, setlists, and music data never leave your device and are never sent to any server.

This website (stagia.app) uses the anonymous audience-measurement tools described below.

InBeat, GigFlow Live and GigFlow Editor apps

This section covers the Android apps InBeat: Live Tempo Manager (com.stagia.inbeat) and GigFlow Live: Music Manager (com.stagia.gigflow.live), as well as the desktop app GigFlow Editor.

  • No data collected. The apps do not collect, transmit, or share any personal or usage data. They contain no analytics tools, no ads, no third-party measurement SDKs.
  • 100% offline. No connection to any server is ever established. Your songs, setlists, tempos, and settings are stored locally on your device only.
  • No account. No sign-up, no login, no email address is required to use the apps.
  • No sensitive permissions. The apps access neither your location, your contacts, the microphone, the camera, nor your files beyond the imports/exports you trigger yourself.
  • You stay in control. Export features (JSON, .inb, or HTML files) are triggered manually by you and produce files that remain your responsibility. Uninstalling an app deletes all of its local data.

In short: your music data belongs to you and never leaves your device.

Data collected on this website

stagia.app uses the following tools for anonymous audience measurement:

Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

Audience measurement: pages visited, session duration, approximate geographic origin. Data is processed in aggregated form for statistical purposes; Google may nonetheless process technical identifiers (IP address, device identifiers) as part of this measurement. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Google Tag Manager (GTM)

Tag management tool. GTM itself does not collect data — it orchestrates the triggering of GA4. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

No other data is collected. There are no advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking, no account-creation form.

Cookies

This site uses audience-measurement cookies (Google Analytics), which are not necessary for the site to function and are subject to your consent: a banner lets you accept or decline on your first visit, and Google Analytics only runs if you accept. No advertising cookies are used. You can also block these cookies via your browser settings or a blocking extension (uBlock Origin, etc.).

Retention period

Anonymous browsing data collected by Google Analytics is kept for 14 months, in line with the retention setting configured in our Google Analytics account, then automatically deleted.

Your rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — know what data is collected about you
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your data
  • Right to object — object to the processing of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@stagia.app. You can also file a complaint with the CNIL, France's data protection authority, or with your own country's equivalent authority.
