Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

General principles

Stagia takes protecting your privacy seriously. The GigFlow Live and InBeat Android apps run entirely offline, and the web tools (GigFlow Editor, InBeat in your browser) open in your browser with no account — in every case, your songs, setlists, and music data never leave your device and are never sent to any server.

This website (stagia.app) uses, only with your consent, the audience-measurement tools described below.

InBeat, GigFlow Live and GigFlow Editor apps

This section covers the Android apps InBeat: Live Tempo Manager (com.stagia.inbeat) and GigFlow Live: Music Manager (com.stagia.gigflow.live), as well as the GigFlow Editor and InBeat in your browser web tools.

  • No data collected by the Android apps. InBeat and GigFlow Live do not collect, transmit, or share any personal or usage data. They contain no analytics tools, no ads, no third-party measurement SDKs. The GigFlow Editor and InBeat in your browser web tools, hosted on stagia.app, are part of the website: their audience measurement is described in the "Data collected on this website" section. This difference is deliberate: no collection in the Android apps, audience measurement subject to your consent on the web.
  • Your music data stays with you. The GigFlow Live and InBeat Android apps run 100% offline. GigFlow Editor and InBeat in your browser never send your songs, setlists, tempos, or settings to any server. They are stored locally on your device only (in the browser for GigFlow Editor and InBeat in your browser, using the browser's local storage and, for InBeat, a copy of the app kept for offline use). A browser may erase this data; export it regularly.
  • No account. No sign-up, no login, no email address is required to use the apps.
  • No sensitive permissions. The apps access neither your location, your contacts, the microphone, the camera, nor your files beyond the imports/exports you trigger yourself.
  • You stay in control. Export features (JSON, .inb, or HTML files) are triggered manually by you and produce files that remain your responsibility. Uninstalling an app deletes all of its local data.

In short: your music data belongs to you and never leaves your device.

Data collected on this website

stagia.app, including the GigFlow Editor and InBeat in your browser web tools, uses the following tools for audience measurement (aggregated statistics based on pseudonymised data). For GigFlow Editor, only the opening of the tool and the type of export performed (backup, songs, setlists, chord library, or setlist PDF) are counted; for InBeat in your browser, only the opening of the web version and adding it to your home screen (once per device) are counted. In both cases, these counts are made only if you accepted audience measurement in the banner (legal basis: your consent): never the content of your songs or setlists.

Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

Audience measurement: pages visited, session duration, approximate geographic origin. Results are provided to us as aggregated statistics; Google, however, processes pseudonymised data on Stagia's behalf (cookie identifier, IP address, device and browser) as part of this measurement. This data is collected only after you accept. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Google Tag Manager (GTM)

Tag management tool. GTM itself does not collect data — it orchestrates the triggering of GA4. It is loaded only after you accept. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

YouTube — embedded videos

Some pages of the site (for example an overview video or tutorials) embed videos hosted on Stagia's YouTube channel, using the enhanced privacy mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Videos are loaded only after you click "Play video"; before that click, no connection is made to YouTube. Once a video is loaded, your browser connects to YouTube (Google) servers, which may process your IP address and use cookies or technical identifiers under their own policy. Stagia receives no personal data as a result. These videos apply to the website only: the Android apps embed none. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Google Play — download links

The "Google Play" buttons on the InBeat pages open the app's Google Play page, with a parameter indicating that you come from stagia.app. Google Play processes your visit under its own policy; Stagia only receives aggregated statistics (number of installs by origin) through the developer console. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

GitHub Pages — hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). The host receives your IP address on every visit and keeps it in its technical logs under its own policy, regardless of your choice in the banner. Privacy policy: docs.github.com

Transfers outside the European Union. Google (GA4, GTM, YouTube, Google Play) and GitHub are based in the United States. These transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR (the EU–US Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision for certified providers, or standard contractual clauses), depending on the provider; their privacy policies give the details.

No other data is collected by Stagia. Stagia uses no advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking and no account-creation form; only the embedded YouTube videos above fall under Google's policy.

Cookies

This site uses audience-measurement cookies (Google Analytics), which are not necessary for the site to function and are subject to your consent: a banner lets you accept or decline on your first visit. If you accept, Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics are loaded and measurement cookies are set. These cookies (_ga and _ga_ followed by an identifier) have a lifetime of 13 months from your first visit; it is not extended by later visits. If you decline, nothing is loaded or sent to Google for audience measurement and no measurement cookie is set. You can change your mind at any time with the "Manage cookies" link at the bottom of every page, or here: Manage cookies; withdrawing your consent deletes the measurement cookies. No advertising cookies are set by Stagia; embedded YouTube videos may, however, set their own cookies during playback (see above). You can also block these cookies via your browser settings or a blocking extension (uBlock Origin, etc.). In addition, GigFlow Editor and InBeat in your browser use your browser's local storage (and, for InBeat, an app cache for offline mode) to keep your data and settings; this storage is necessary for the tools to work, stays on your device and is not used for any tracking.

Retention period

Pseudonymised browsing data collected by Google Analytics (after you accept) is kept for 14 months, in line with the retention setting configured in our Google Analytics account, then automatically deleted.

Your rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — know what data is collected about you
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your data
  • Right to object — object to the processing of your data
  • Right to restriction — ask for a temporary freeze on processing
  • Right to portability — get your data in a common format, where processing is based on your consent
  • Withdrawal of consent — at any time, with the "Manage cookies" link, without affecting what was done before withdrawal
  • Post-mortem directives — define what happens to your data after your death

To exercise these rights (we reply within one month), contact us at contact@stagia.app. You can also file a complaint with the CNIL, France's data protection authority, or with your own country's equivalent authority.
