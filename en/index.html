The GigFlow signature

Every chord has its color. Every section, its marker.

A row of colored pills at the top of the screen represents each part of the song. One tap to jump to the intro, the chorus, the bridge — without losing your place. Sections are never split across two pages.

Readable at arm's length or from a meter away, in rehearsal or under direct sun. Dark by default, light and high-contrast when the stage calls for it. Chord-based lyric coloring — like many other settings — can be freely switched on or off to match your preferences.
