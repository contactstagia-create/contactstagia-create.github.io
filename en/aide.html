Pricing

GigFlow Live is free to start — you can create songs and setlists without paying. Unlimited access is unlocked with a one-time purchase, no subscription, no ads, no surprises. The purchase is made directly through the Google Play Store — billing is entirely handled by Google Play.
No. InBeat and GigFlow Editor are entirely free, unlimited, and with no subscription.

Usage

In GigFlow Editor, use the Export button to generate a file. Transfer it to your Android device (via cable, Bluetooth, e-mail…) then import it into GigFlow Live or InBeat from the app's import menu.
Yes. GigFlow Live includes a quick editor for last-minute tweaks — a chord to fix, a lyric to adjust. For full preparation, GigFlow Editor on PC offers more comfort (keyboard, mouse, big screen).
GigFlow Live and InBeat are compatible with Bluetooth pedals. One press = next song (or next section, depending on your setup). You keep your hands free while you play.
