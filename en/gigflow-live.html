Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you hit the stage.

Does GigFlow Live work without an internet connection?

Yes. GigFlow Live is 100% offline: no connection required, no account to create. Your setlists and repertoire stay stored on your phone.

How do I import setlists I prepared on PC?

Prepare your songs and setlist in GigFlow Editor, then import the file into GigFlow Live in one click — no retyping. You can also build everything directly in Live on mobile or tablet, but Editor stays more comfortable for full setlist prep.

Do I need a subscription?

No. GigFlow Live unlocks with a one-time purchase after the free trial — never a subscription, never recurring fees.

Can I turn pages without touching the screen?

Yes, via a compatible Bluetooth pedal — handy when your hands are busy with your instrument.

Is GigFlow Live compatible with InBeat?

Yes. Both apps share the Stagia ecosystem: each song's tempo can be loaded automatically into InBeat from your GigFlow Live setlist.
