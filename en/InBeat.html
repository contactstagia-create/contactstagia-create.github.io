The essentials

The right vibe for every song.

Preset tempos

Every song loads its BPM automatically. Nothing to adjust on stage.

Dark or light

Choose your theme — readable indoors or in bright sunlight.

Show timing

100% free, no subscription · 100% offline · no ads · your data stays on your phone.

Library

Your whole repertoire, in one place.

Every song keeps its tempo, time signature, and duration. Search, find, and pull up any song in two taps.

Preparation

Everything is set before the show.

Edit each song — time signature, BPM, duration, key, notes — then drag and drop your songs into show order, with venue, date, and total duration. InBeat then loads each tempo automatically. Prepare it ahead of time on your phone, or right before the show.

Live mode

Big, readable, distraction-free.

Full-screen display. Big BPM and time signature, next song always visible — you anticipate without losing your place. Dark or light theme depending on stage lighting. InBeat automatically loads the tempo of the next song.

  • Bluetooth pedal or swipe — hands-free navigation between songs
  • Tuning fork — plays the reference note to help singers find their pitch
  • Tap tempo — adjust the tempo by ear, on the fly

Visual beat & beep

The downbeat jumps out at you.

Strong and weak beats visually distinguished — red LED on the downbeat, amber on the following beats. An optional audio beep doubles the cue, at the user's choice. Subdivisions can be enabled, all 11 time signatures supported.

  • Hardware-style LED: red on beat 1, amber on 2·3·4
  • Optional audio beep — visual only or visual + sound
  • Tap tempo to lock in by ear

Import & export

Your data, always with you.

Back up, share with the band, or prepare on a big screen: InBeat speaks the same language as the whole Stagia ecosystem.

Export .inb

Export your library and setlists in .inb format. Back up, switch phones, or share with the rest of the band.

Import .inb

Receive an .inb file and import it: songs, tempos, time signatures, and durations arrive already set up.

Export PDF

Generate a PDF of your setlist — venue, date, order, BPM, and durations — to print or send before the show.

Compatible with GigFlow

Same format shared with GigFlow Live and Editor: prepare on PC, play on mobile, no retyping.
